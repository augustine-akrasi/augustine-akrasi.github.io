About

I have taught mathematics in Ghana for nine years, from National Service through to a permanent post with the Ghana Education Service. I teach Core and Elective Mathematics at Apesua Community Senior High School in the Eastern Region, where I also coordinate the Professional Learning Community and train mentors. From 2023 to 2026 I led the school's Mathematics Department.

In 2026 I completed an MSc in Data Science at Koforidua Technical University. My research uses machine learning to study resource equity among senior high schools in Ghana. I also founded AAKIVES Consult, which offers research and data analysis support, online mathematics tuition, and training for teachers on using AI responsibly in lesson planning and assessment.

I am preparing for doctoral study in Mathematics Education and am open to remote research, data and teaching opportunities.
